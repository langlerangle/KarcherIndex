KARCHERINDEX — GUIDE TO THE COMPARED STATEMENTS AND DEFINITIONS

This guide suggests a reading order for the five statements selected by
comparator.json and the definitions they use. Paths are relative to the repository
root. Search for declaration names rather than relying on line numbers.

Start with Palomar/CombinedChallenge.lean. Its five deliberate sorry placeholders
state the obligations to be supplied by the Solution; they are not proofs.
Palomar/Solution.lean re-exports the proofs from Palomar/CombinedSolution.lean.
For project scope, verification evidence and review status, see README.md and
formalization.yaml.

1. Read the five compared statements

All five have the namespace prefix KSPalomar.Intrinsic.Full:

  exists_intrinsic_index_nullity_m2
    For every k >= 3 there exists a realization of the two-period quotient
    with index 4k - 3 and bounded Jacobi-field nullity 3.

  exists_intrinsic_staircase_k3
    For k = 3, one base realization and one threshold serve all positive periods,
    with compatible quotient witnesses satisfying the index and nullity formulas.

  unique_threshold
    Exactly one real parameter s satisfies IsThreshold.

  decimal_bounds
    Every parameter satisfying IsThreshold gives a criticalPhase strictly between
    0.4323617 and 0.4323619.

  period_arithmetic
    For positive periods m <= 2298, the certified interval determines the floor
    of m times criticalPhase and excludes integral m times criticalPhase.

Read the precise hypotheses and quantifiers in the declarations. In particular,
the staircase statement uses one threshold for all periods, and the numerical
statements apply to any parameter satisfying the same IsThreshold predicate.
No irrationality or exact-value claim follows from the finite arithmetic range.

2. Follow the geometric definitions

In CombinedChallenge.lean, locate CoveredSmoothSection, IsBasedEWMapSphere and
CompatibleProjectionAtlas. Read the structure fields and map/chart conditions,
then follow CoveredSmoothSection.index and CoveredSmoothSection.nullity to
geometricIndex, compactSmoothDomain, formIndex, geometricNullity and
boundedGeometricJacobi.

The index counts complex dimensions of negative subspaces of compactly supported
smooth test functions. Nullity counts complex dimensions of bounded smooth
Jacobi fields. These are the paper's complex-valued-function conventions.
Distinguish the construction of an explicit Enneper-Weierstrass realization
from a uniqueness or classification theorem for surfaces; no such uniqueness
claim is made here.

3. Follow the shared spectral threshold

Read sigP, sigM, chat, convS, MemDom, normSq, Qform and gam, then IsThreshold
and criticalPhase. These define the circle variational problem and the phase
used in both the geometric staircase and the numerical statements. Read the
mathematical explanations in the Challenge alongside the definitions; follow
CombinedSolution.lean to see how the library proofs supply these statements.

4. Compare the paper and the formal statements

Use the pinned arXiv v2 paper cited in README.md. Check the geometric theorem
hypotheses, equality cases and scope qualifications. The paper describes its
0.4323618(1) computation as heuristic; the formalization proves a strict enclosure
and its finite-period arithmetic consequences. Consult the README's fidelity
section and formalization.yaml for omissions and differences of proof route.

For further proof navigation, use the README's guide to the development. This
file is a reading guide, not an additional verification certificate.
