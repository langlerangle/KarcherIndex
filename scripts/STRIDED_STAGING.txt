Reproducing the ProdEnergyP staging in this candidate

After regenerating the original ProdEnergyP0..256 sources with negsign_generator.py, run:
  python3 scripts/stride_stage.py --repo . --leaf-start 0 --leaf-count 257 --width 8
The transform refuses already-staged input. It adds one private predecessor import to each
leaf 8..256, leaving proof bodies and public glue unchanged. There are eight residue chains.
This bounds concurrent builds in this family to eight; it does not limit all Lake tasks.
Do not pass these private imports through the generator's public-import header rewriting.
The transform was measured on 16 vCPU / 32 GB; full candidate verification is separate.
