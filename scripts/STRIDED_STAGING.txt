ProdEnergyP compilation ordering and regeneration boundary

The committed ProdEnergyP0..256 family has sixteen predecessor-import chains.
Heads 0..7 lead through 56..63. Heads 64..71 restart the chains, which extend
through 256. Every non-head module imports its predecessor eight indices earlier.
The selected Solution uses only ProdEnergyP64..191, through
AlphaEnergy4096FinitePCenter: eight chains with heads 64..71. Their restart
keeps the discarded lower blocks out of the selected import closure.
This limits concurrent compilation within this family, not all Lake tasks.

On a separate copy of the original sources regenerated with negsign_generator.py:
  python3 scripts/stride_stage.py --repo . --leaf-start 0 --leaf-count 257 --width 8
The transform refuses already-staged input. This full-range command adds one
private predecessor import to each leaf 8..256, leaving proof bodies and public
glue unchanged. It creates eight chains WITHOUT the committed restart at 64;
its output therefore does not reproduce the committed staging or optimized proofs.
In the committed sources, the predecessor imports at 64..71 are absent. Do not
restore them: that would bring the lower blocks into the selected Solution closure.
Do not pass these private imports through the generator's public-import header rewriting.
The original full-family transform was measured on 16 vCPU / 32 GB; that measurement
is separate from verification of the current selected proofs.
